# Apple Accessibility Guidance

For web testing, use Safari and VoiceOver with real devices. For native apps, verify accessibility labels, traits, values, hints, grouping, and focus with platform-specific tooling and APIs. Do not assume a web ARIA mapping directly transfers to UIKit or SwiftUI.

References: https://developer.apple.com/accessibility/ and https://support.apple.com/guide/voiceover/welcome/mac
